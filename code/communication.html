Parts of Speech
  • Noun - Place, name, thing, profession
  • Adjective - Describes or modifies a Noun.
  • Verb - Action words or states of being.
  • Adverb - Describes or modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb.
  • Gerund - Verb + "-ing" that acts as a Noun - e.g., Swimming is good exercise.
  • Pronoun - A word that substitutes for a main noun (e.g., he, she, it, they).
  • Conjunction - Connecting words within a sentence (e.g., and, or, but).
  • Transitions - Words connecting sentences or thoughts within a paragraph (e.g., However [contrasting], Therefore [cause-effect]).
Figure of Speech
  • Simile - Compare two different things using "like" or "as" - uvamai ani - Indirect comparison - She swims like a fish.
  • Metaphor - Compare two different things & straight to the point - uruvagam - Direct comparison - This classroom is a fish market.
  • Analogy - Comparing one thing to another for explanation or clarification - Run like a horse to succeed. (Note: Analogy is a logical argument or comparison technique, though it often uses similes or metaphors).
  • Irony - வஞ்சப்புகழ்ச்சி - Contrast between what is said & what is meant - After having the worst day, telling peers "Wow, what a great day of my life."
  • Sarcasm - Kindal - Satirist - A sharp, mocking, or ironic remark intended to convey contempt or humor.
