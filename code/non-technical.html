Income Tax
  • Income tax is a direct tax levied by the government on the annual earnings of individuals, businesses, and other legal entities.
  • Marginal Relief - Prevents high tax on small excess income which you upgrade to next slab
  • 0 to 4 lpa - 0%
  • 4-8 lpa - 5%
  • 8-12lpa - 10%
  • 12-16 lpa - 15%
  • 24+lpa - 30%
  • Each year we have an old & a new tax regime
  • old tax regime - high tax rates - we get back the deduction using valid claims listed like insurance
  • New tax regime - Lower tax rates - most deduction removed - standard 75k is allowed
  • TDS - Tax deducted at Source - Your employers deducts the tax before giving your salary & deposit the amount behalf of the employee | If TDS is deducted too much -> File ITR -> Income Tax Return Rebate | Else you have pay the difference
  • Deadlines - July 31 for salaried | August 31 for freelancers | April 01 0001 to march 31 0002 -> 365 days calculated
Form 16
  • Form 16 (now renumbered as Form 130 from Tax Year 2026–27) is an annual tax certificate issued by your employer by June 15 showing your total salary and TDS deposited with the government. When you switch jobs mid-year, you will collect one certificate from Company A and another from Company B after the financial year ends. If you submitted Form 122 to Company B earlier, their certificate will already factor in your combined annual tax, and you will use both certificates together to file your Income Tax Return.
  • No TDS - No form 16
  • Issued by company to employee | Official Document that hold your salary, deduction, record, declared by the employer | ITR filing key Document | For the April 1 2025 to March 31 2026 -> The employer should release the form 16 on or before june 15 2026
Form 26AS
  • Your tax passbook - Shows all TDS credited against your PAN Card from all sources
  • 12B form - Mid Financial year job change | 5 months in company A & 7 months in company B
  • Form 12B is prepared and filled out by the employee, not the employer.
  • Once you leave Company A, collect your final payslips, Full & Final settlement statement, and Form 26AS from the income tax portal. Download Form 122 online (or use your new company's HR portal) and fill in your personal details, previous salary, and TDS deducted by Company A. Sign the form and submit it along with your previous payslip copies to Company B's HR/payroll team so they can accurately calculate your remaining tax for the year.
  • Form 12B is old, now its Form 122
